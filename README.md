# Don't Tap

Don't Tap the White Tile in your RuneLite sidebar. Tap the black tiles as fast
as you can, and never tap a white one. Great for warming up your clicks
between kills without alt-tabbing to a browser.

Same three modes and rules as donttap.com:

- **Endurance**: you start with 10 seconds, and every 40 taps earns 10 more.
  Keep a streak going to fill the gauge and set off a power-up that fills the
  whole board.
- **Frenzy**: 30 seconds, nothing more. A fuller gauge makes every tap worth
  more, up to x5.
- **Pattern**: clear 15 patterns of 4 tiles as fast as you can.

Tapping a white tile ends the round. Taps count the moment the mouse button
goes down, just like the original.

Play in the sidebar, or pop the game out into its own resizable window for
bigger tiles. The window remembers where you left it. Your best score for each
mode is saved, and clicking the board after a round starts the next one.

## Options

- Skip the countdown to start rounds instantly
- Tap sounds (off by default) with a volume slider
